Provincial comparative search, 12 October 2026

Official sources downloaded: USask January30 2025 Council agenda, University of
Manitoba February5 2025 Senate minutes, McMaster May16 2025 Presidents Awards
account. Evidence extracts preserve each institution's definition. They are
selected examples, not a provincial census.

USask: agenda PDF page1024 (Provost report page1) reports2958 allocated in2024,
1623 endorsed as ofDecember3, and approximately55% utilization. Endorsement is
not necessarily submission to IRCC. Manitoba p4 gives7540 allocated and fewer
than half used, not an exact usage count. McMaster gives90%issued and70%usage
without an absolute denominator or detailed definition.

BC searches: ministry international education page, March2024 release,
institution accountability/budget PDFs, legislative debate March3 2026 and
Finance committee June5 2025 yielded quotas/procedures/permits, not provincial
actual PAL issuance totals.

Nova Scotia: March2024 allocation attachment, DLI instructions, bilateral
agreements and the2024-25 Advanced Education accountability report were checked.
No PAL issuance count located. MSVU May22 2024 Senate minutes say issuance
figures were reported but do not reproduce the numbers.

Ontario: no official province-wide usage table found in this search. A
January13 2026 secondary article reproduces Toronto Star FOI reporting, but
the underlying disclosure was not located. Those figures are not treated as
independently verified primary data or copied into the verified metric files.
