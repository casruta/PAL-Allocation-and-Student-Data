PUBLIC ATIP SUMMARY SEARCH — collected 2026-10-12

This folder contains discovery evidence, not the underlying disclosed records.

What was collected
The complete public federal ATI summary CSV (116,634,003 bytes; 204,988 rows) was downloaded unchanged to raw/atip/ati-all.csv. The filter searched the 7,750 IRCC request summaries completed in 2020 or later, using English and French student-related terms. It retained 592 broad student-related summaries and five narrowly PAL-related summaries. Fourteen priority summary pages were also downloaded and checked for the matching request ID. Hashes and URLs are in provenance.json and record_download_receipts.json. Run filter_atip.ps1 to reproduce the extracts.

The broad extract intentionally includes irrelevant keyword hits; it is a discovery index rather than a fully adjudicated list of student datasets. The five PAL matches were read and are genuinely related to provincial attestation letters, but one concerns processing guidance rather than numerical allocations or usage.

Record URLs and exact original English/French summaries are in priority_record_leads.csv.

Access limitation
These summary pages identify existing released packages but do not publish their contents as attached files in the pages saved here. No copies were requested, no forms submitted and no email sent. IRCC's completed-requests page explains that copies of previously released records can be requested at no cost. User authorization would be required before sending a request. The retained request numbers are a concrete follow-up route, not claims that these records have already been obtained.
https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/transparency/access-information-privacy/completed-requests.html
Supporting quote: "you can request a copy of the records previously released at no cost."

Reading cautions
- Completion year/month differs from the period requested in the summary.
- The pages column counts pages, not students, permits, or PALs.
- 'All disclosed' is the release disposition. It does not guarantee that every requested detail was held or supplied.
- IRCC study-permit decisions are not enrolment, and PAL-linked decisions are not all letters issued by a province.
- No PAL records before the program's 2024 introduction should be treated as missing annual usage observations.
- The public ATI catalogue omits personal/proprietary requests and does not capture every federal institution's summaries; keyword searching also has limits.

Not accepted as usage evidence
UCP2025 convention resolution22 calls 40,894 'PALs issued'. This is a political-party resolution, and 40,894 coincides with the federal application allocation already in the collection. It is not accepted as evidence of actual issued letters.
https://unitedconservative.ca/wp-content/uploads/Resolutions2025AGM.pdf

Search conclusion
This search did not obtain a public Alberta university-by-university PAL-issuance/usage table. It found specific previously completed access-to-information records that can materially improve institution-level permit/allocation coverage, plus precise public documentation of the provincial data custodian. The conclusion is 'not obtained from the searched public sources', not 'the data does not exist'.
