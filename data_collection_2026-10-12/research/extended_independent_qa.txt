MEASURE AND DATE DISTINCTIONS REQUIRED IN ANY DOWNSTREAM ANSWER
IRCC older year-end permit workbook: December31 valid-permit stock, with
Alberta classified by intended destination. Focused coverage2020-2024. It does
not prove attendance or physical presence at a particular university.

IRCC permit-effective-year tables: holders grouped by when permits became
effective, not December31 stock and not new-arrival counts. Complete calendar
2020-2025 plus January-July2026. Do not annualize2026 without an explicit model.

IRCC current monthly stock: December2023-July2026, month-end dates. Source says
'These totals do not include asylum claimants, protected persons or related
groups who may hold a study or work permit.' Dual work/study holders count as
study holders. July31,2026 value590,205 is an as-of stock, not a full-year flow.
Its December2024 figure928,220 differs from996,375 in the older year-end
workbook; scope/vintage differ and these series must not be spliced.

IRCC new arrivals: excludes permit extensions and asylum claimants. Simultaneous
study/work permits count under study. Annual2024 and2025 are complete;2026
explicitly January-July. Source rounds counts above5 to multiples of5, suppresses
0-5 as '--', and identifies its data date as July31,2026.

Statistics Canada PSIS table37-10-0086: academic2019/20-2024/25, but a fall
snapshot chosen between September30 and December1. Counts program enrolments,
not unique people or all enrolments during the academic year. Institutions
and totals are hierarchical categories, not additive peers. Random rounding
to multiples of3 explains Alberta2024/25 total41,007 versus university19,461
plus college21,543 (=41,004). Do not force component agreement. This source's
observed2024/25 release supersedes the same year's earlier modelled estimate
in the separately preserved May2026 feasibility study. Prairies != Alberta.

Statistics Canada NPR table17-10-0121: quarterly usual-resident estimates,
July1,2021-July1,2026; not institutional enrolment. Categories are mutually
exclusive under an asylum/protected/related-first hierarchy. Metadata marks
periods through July1,2023 final; October1,2023-April1,2026 updated; July1,2026
preliminary. Preserve STATUS and date. A July1,2026 snapshot is not an annual
2026 count; the source is not a substitute for a full2020 series.

Alberta LERS: annual unique headcount at each published aggregation level,
academic2019/20-2024/25 in the focus folder. Legal status Student Visa or Other
Visa; only provincially approved programming at publicly funded reporting
institutions. Banff Centre excluded. Does not represent all private, K-12 or
language-school students. Some refugee/work-permit apprentices are reported
as international under current LERS rules. Institutional totals must not be
summed to estimate unique system total; published system total already dedupes.
Its annual headcount is distinct from PSIS fall program enrolment and IRCC stock.

LERS longitudinal breaks are material: Athabasca2024/25 spans January2024-
April2025 and artificially increases totals; the noted7,500extra learners are
all learners, not an international-only adjustment. NorQuest repeats spring2024
across2023/24 and2024/25; MaKami first reports2024/25. Earlier MRU/NAIT/SAIT,
Northwestern and Lakeland reporting changes also repeat sessions. Preserve the
entire reporting-changes file rather than treating all annual movements as
changes in demand. Remote Athabasca enrolment does not establish Alberta residence.

PAL EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
PALs begin2024: earlier years are not applicable, not zero usage. Provincial
application-space allocations and expected permit issuances are capacities or
targets, not actual issued PALs, IRCC submissions, approvals, arrivals or enrolment.

Alberta fiscal2024-25 portal issued26,089 (annual report physical PDFp58).
Do not divide by a calendar-year allocation to compute use.
Lethbridge initial allocation2,687 covers Jan22,2024-Jan21,2025 (budgetp3).
Lethbridge later reports1,784issued 'last year' in Feb2025 minutes(p2), while
the fees report(p54) says it does not know how many used them to apply. These
do not establish a matched PAL-submission utilization percentage. Fall307
registrants mentioned in source material are not a matched1784-PAL cohort.
MacEwan1,500 is an expected2025 allocation (Feb2025 minutesp2), not confirmed
allocation or issuance. Calgary discloses underuse without a numeric numerator
or denominator. All three stages must remain distinct: allocated, issued, used.
