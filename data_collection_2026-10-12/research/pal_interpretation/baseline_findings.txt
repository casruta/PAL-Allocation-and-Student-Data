IMPORTANT MISLEADING SOURCE WORDING
2024 Annual Report to Parliament says 682,889 permit holders "entered Canada" in2023. Its five-year totals are close to permit-effective series including extensions and are NOT directly comparable with new-arrival measures. Do not use that headline as a count of first/new arrivals. Prefer exact named measures above.
