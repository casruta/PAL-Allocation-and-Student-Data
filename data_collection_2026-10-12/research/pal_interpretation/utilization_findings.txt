Main finding
There is evidence of underused allocation at Calgary, but no verified complete table of Alberta university allocation utilization. Missing exact rates must remain unknown, not zero. PAL allocation is capacity to submit eligible study-permit applications, not an admissions target or count of students arriving. University admissions, student acceptance/deposit, PAL issuance, IRCC submission, approval, arrival and registration are separate stages.

At Calgary, December12 2024 CCS minutes p2 say PALs were issued after offer acceptance and deposit; January22 2025 GAPS minutes p2 report approximately40% lower international applications in2024 and unfilled allocation. This is direct institutional evidence of weaker applications, not an independently identified causal impact of PAL alone. GAPS source quote total in CSV is25 words across the two rows; preserve source-level25-word limit in final citation quotes.

At Lethbridge,1784PALs issued 'last year' is February3 2025 GFC p2. Initial2687allocation explicitly spans January22 2024-January21 2025, from March1 2024 budget message p3. The tempting1784/2687=66.4% is NOT a verified sameperiod utilization rate, so omit it from headline rates. Even a sameperiod issuance ratio would not equal submission or arrival conversion. Fees2025-26 report p54 explicitly says actual submission counts unknown; its 'over1000'PAL counterfactual and307Fall2024 registrations do not create a valid conversion cohort. It describes those307 as51.5%new high-school and48.5%transfer undergraduates, with top origins India/Ghana/Nigeria/Kenya/China; this describes that registration group, not all Alberta PAL recipients. Keep quotes from this source to<=25words in final prose.

2.
