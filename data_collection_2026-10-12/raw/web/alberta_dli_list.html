Designated post-secondary institutions

Find out which post-secondary institutions in Alberta are designated to enrol international students.

Skip to On this page

Overview

If you plan to apply for a study permit in Canada, you will need a letter of acceptance to a program offered by a designated post-secondary institution.

Designation requirements are guided by the Government of Canada's International Student Program, see Canada’s Designated learning institutions list.

Find out how post-secondary institutions are designated in Alberta.

This list of designated institutions in Alberta is updated on a regular basis.

Institutions listed can host international students on study permits.
